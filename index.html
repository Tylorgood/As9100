Assessment to strategy

We don't guess. We follow the work.

We examine actual jobs and production records, compare the evidence against applicable requirements, and translate findings into an executable plan.

01 / BASELINE

Walk the shop

EXAMPLE FINDING

Which CNC program revision made this part?

Programs are stored in multiple locations, and records don't consistently establish the approved revision used.

Strategy: Control the master program library, release authority, revisions, and change approvals.

Implementation: Pilot the workflow on real jobs and train operators.

Proof: Verify approved program use through sampled production records.

Illustrative only; findings depend on the actual assessment.

FAQ

Clear expectations.

Is this an AS9100 certification audit?

No. We support implementation and readiness. An accredited certification body independently conducts the formal certification audit.

Does every shop need identical documentation?

No. The roadmap depends on the shop's actual processes, customer requirements, and scope.

Can we be certified in 12 weeks?

That is not guaranteed. Implementation timing depends on the baseline; certification also depends on evidence, registrar scheduling, and audit outcomes.
